What is PDF to JPG Converter?

PDF to JPG converter is a free online tool that converts each page of your PDF document into a separate high-quality JPG image. In India, we often need to convert PDF to JPG for many reasons - like uploading Aadhaar or PAN as JPG on government portals (Scholarship, Passport, Job forms), sending a single page of a PDF book on WhatsApp, or posting a PDF page on Instagram.

Many websites like iLovePDF convert PDF on their server. That means your file is uploaded. But Antokia's tool is different - it works completely inside your browser using pdf.js. Your file never leaves your mobile or laptop. This is very important for private documents like Aadhaar, PAN card, bank statement.

How Antokia Conversion Works?
PDF File (in your device) → pdf.js Reads Pages → Canvas Rendering at 2x Scale → High Quality JPG Export (92% quality, ~300 DPI) → Download
No file is sent to server. Everything happens offline in your browser.
