What Is a Discount?

A discount is a reduction from the original price of a product or service. Discounts are commonly offered during sales, promotions, festivals and special offers.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount
Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100
Final Price
Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example of Discount Calculation

Original Price
₹1,000
Discount
20%
Discount Amount
₹1,000 × 20 ÷ 100 = ₹200
Final Price
₹800

How to Calculate Discount Manually

  1. Note the original price of the product.
  2. Note the discount percentage.
  3. Multiply the original price by the discount percentage.
  4. Divide the result by 100 to get the discount amount.
  5. Subtract the discount amount from the original price to get the final price.

Where Is Discount Calculation Used?

  • Shopping and online purchases.
  • Festival and seasonal sales.
  • Comparing product prices.
  • Business and retail pricing.
  • Calculating sale prices.
Tip:

Always check whether a displayed sale price already includes the discount before applying another discount.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a 20% discount?
Multiply the original price by 20 and divide by 100. The result is the discount amount.
What is the formula for discount?
Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100.
How do I calculate the final price after discount?
Subtract the discount amount from the original price.
Can I use this calculator for shopping discounts?
Yes. You can use it to quickly calculate discount amounts and final prices for shopping and sale offers.
