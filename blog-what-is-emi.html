What is EMI and How to Calculate it? (2026 Guide)

Updated: May 2026 | By Antokia Team

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is a fixed amount you pay every month to repay your loan. If you have taken any loan, you can easily calculate it with our free EMI Calculator.

EMI Formula

The formula to calculate EMI is very simple:

EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]

  • P = Loan Amount (Principal)
  • R = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100)
  • N = Total Number of Months

Example of EMI Calculation

Suppose you take a loan of Rs 5,00,000 for 5 years at 10% annual interest.

P = 500000, R = 10/12/100 = 0.00833, N = 60 months
EMI = Rs 10,624 per month (approx)
