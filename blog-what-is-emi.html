What is EMI and How to Calculate it?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed amount you pay to bank every month...

EMI Formula

EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]

P = Loan Amount, R = Monthly Interest Rate, N = Tenure in months

Example

If you take 10 lakh loan at 8% for 20 years, your EMI will be around Rs 8364.
