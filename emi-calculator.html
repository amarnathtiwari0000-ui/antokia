What is an EMI Calculator?

An EMI Calculator is a financial planning tool that helps you estimate the monthly instalment payable on a loan. By entering the loan amount, annual interest rate and loan tenure, you can calculate the estimated monthly EMI, total interest and total amount payable over the selected loan period.

The calculator can be used for hypothetical home loan, personal loan, car loan and other similar repayment calculations. The result is an estimate and may differ from the actual repayment schedule provided by a lender.

EMI Calculator Formula

The calculator uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula to estimate the monthly loan instalment.

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ [(1 + r)ⁿ − 1]
P = Loan principal amount
r = Monthly interest rate
n = Total number of monthly instalments
EMI = Estimated monthly instalment
