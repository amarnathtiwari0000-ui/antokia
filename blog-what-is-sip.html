What is SIP and How to Calculate Returns?

SIP means Systematic Investment Plan. You invest fixed amount monthly in Mutual Funds. Calculate with our SIP Calculator.

Formula: M = P x ({[1+i]^n -1} / i) x (1+i)

Example: Rs 5000 monthly for 10 years at 12% = ~ Rs 11.6 Lakh
