What is Image Compressor Tool?

Free Image Compressor by Antokia is used to reduce image size from MB to KB. If you need to upload a photo for a government job form, college admission, or SSC form where limit is 20KB, 50KB or 100KB, this tool compresses your JPG, PNG, WEBP instantly. Unlike other sites, we compress images inside your browser (offline), so your private photos are never uploaded to any server. No watermark, no login, unlimited use. It works perfectly on mobile and PC.

How to Compress Image to 20KB / 50KB?

  • Upload your JPG/PNG image.
  • Select Quality - Set 40% for 20KB, 60% for 50KB, 70% for 100KB.
  • Click Compress Now and Download.
