Create QR Codes Online for Free

QR codes provide a convenient way to share information using a smartphone camera. With Antokia's QR Code Generator, you can turn a website URL, text or other information into a scannable QR code.

The generated QR code can be downloaded as a PNG image and used on websites, documents, posters, presentations, printed materials and other suitable applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Antokia's QR Code Generator free?

Yes. The QR Code Generator is available free to use.

Can I create a QR code for a website?

Yes. Enter the complete website URL, such as https://antokia.com/, and generate the QR code.

Can I use text instead of a URL?

Yes. You can enter text or other supported information instead of a website URL.

Can I download my QR code?

Yes. After generating the QR code, use the Download PNG button to save it as an image.

Does the QR code generator require an account?

No account is required to generate a QR code with this tool.
