Free PDF to Excel Converter

Antokia's PDF to Excel Converter extracts readable text and row-based data from PDF files and creates an editable XLSX spreadsheet. It is useful when you need to move information from a text-based PDF into Excel for further editing, sorting or analysis.

The conversion happens directly in your browser. No login is required, making it convenient for quick document processing on desktop and mobile devices.

How to Convert PDF to Excel?

  1. Select or drag and drop your PDF file.
  2. Wait while the PDF pages are processed.
  3. Review the extracted data preview.
  4. Click Download Excel File (.xlsx).
  5. Open the XLSX file in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets or another compatible spreadsheet application.

What Can Be Extracted?

  • Readable text from PDF pages.
  • Text arranged into row-based spreadsheet data.
  • Multi-page PDF content.
  • Basic table-like information from text-based PDFs.

Important: PDF files use many different layouts. Text-based PDFs generally provide better extraction results. Scanned documents, image-only PDFs, complex tables, merged cells, graphics and unusual layouts may not convert into perfectly structured Excel columns and may require manual editing after download.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert PDF to Excel?

Upload a PDF, wait for the data extraction to finish, review the preview and click Download Excel File to create the XLSX spreadsheet.

Is the PDF to Excel converter free?

Yes. Antokia's PDF to Excel converter is free to use.

Can I convert a multi-page PDF?

Yes. The tool processes the pages of the selected PDF and combines the extracted data into one Excel workbook.

Will every PDF table convert perfectly?

Not necessarily. PDF layouts vary significantly. Text-based PDFs generally work better, while scanned PDFs and complex tables may require manual formatting in Excel.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use the tool without registration or login.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

The extraction and Excel creation are performed in your browser and do not require uploading the PDF to an Antokia conversion server.

Antokia provides browser-based utility tools for general document processing. Always review extracted spreadsheet data before using it for official, financial or business purposes.
