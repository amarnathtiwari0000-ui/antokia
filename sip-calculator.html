What is a SIP Calculator?

A SIP Calculator is a financial planning tool that helps you estimate the potential value of regular monthly investments. By entering your monthly SIP amount, expected annual return and investment period, you can calculate the total amount invested, estimated returns and potential maturity value.

The calculator is useful for comparing different hypothetical investment scenarios. It does not predict or guarantee actual mutual fund performance.

SIP Calculator Formula

The calculator uses a standard future-value formula for periodic investments.

M = P × [((1 + r)ⁿ − 1) / r] × (1 + r)
P = Monthly investment amount
r = Monthly rate of return
n = Total number of monthly investments
M = Estimated maturity value
