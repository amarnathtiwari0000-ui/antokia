What is an Age Calculator?

An Age Calculator is an online tool that calculates a person's age from their date of birth. Antokia's free Age Calculator shows the completed years, months and days between the date of birth and the current date.

It can be useful for checking age for birthdays, school or admission forms, applications, documents, eligibility checks and other everyday calculations.

How to Use the Age Calculator

1

Select Date of Birth

Enter your correct date of birth using the calendar field.

2

Click Calculate

Click the Calculate Age button to process your date of birth.

3

View Your Age

Your age will be displayed in years, months and days.

How is Age Calculated?

Age is calculated by comparing the date of birth with the current date. The calculator first determines the completed years, then calculates the remaining months and days.

Age = Current Date − Date of Birth

The result is adjusted according to the number of days in each month and the calendar year.
