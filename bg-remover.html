Free Online Background Remover

Antokia Background Remover is a free online image editing tool designed to help you remove the background from photos and create transparent PNG images. The tool runs the background-removal process in your browser, so you can edit an image without creating an account or uploading the original photo to an Antokia processing server.

After removing the background, you can keep the result transparent or replace the background with white, black, a custom color, a blurred version of the original image, or another image from your device. You can also manually erase unwanted areas or restore parts of the original subject.

How to Remove Background from an Image

  1. Open the Antokia Background Remover.
  2. Click the upload area and select a JPG, PNG or WEBP image.
  3. Wait while the browser-based AI processes the image.
  4. Review the transparent result.
  5. Use Erase or Restore if the edges need manual correction.
  6. Choose Transparent, White, Black, Blur, Color or Image background if required.
  7. Download the final PNG or JPG image.

Supported Image Formats

You can select common image formats including JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP. The transparent result is downloaded as PNG because PNG supports transparent pixels.

Is My Image Uploaded to a Server?

The background-removal processing on this page is performed in your browser using client-side AI libraries. Antokia does not require an account or an image upload to an Antokia image-processing server for this tool. Your browser still downloads the required AI model and supporting libraries from third-party content delivery networks before processing can begin.

Important Image Quality Note

Automatic background removal works best when the main subject is clearly separated from the background. Hair, transparent objects, very similar colors, complicated scenes and small objects can require manual correction. The Erase and Restore tools are provided so you can refine the result before downloading it.
