About Antokia

Simple, useful and accessible online tools for everyday digital tasks. No complicated software and no unnecessary signup.

Welcome to Antokia.com, a free online tools platform designed to make common digital tasks simpler and more accessible. Our goal is to provide useful browser-based tools that people can use without installing complicated software.

Antokia was started in 2026 in Mumbai, India with the idea of making practical digital tools available to students, professionals, creators, businesses and everyday internet users. We focus on simple interfaces, fast loading pages and tools that are easy to understand.

🎯

Our Mission

We want to make useful online utilities easier to access. Antokia focuses on straightforward tools that help users complete everyday tasks quickly from a web browser.

What We Focus On

📱 Mobile Friendly

Privacy & Browser-Based Processing

Some Antokia tools are designed to process files directly in your browser. When a tool works entirely on your device, the file does not need to be uploaded to a server for processing. The exact behavior can vary by tool, so users should review the relevant tool page for specific information.
