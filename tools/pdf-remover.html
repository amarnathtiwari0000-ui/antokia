Free PDF Page Remover

Antokia's PDF Page Remover lets you delete unwanted pages from a PDF document without installing software. Upload your PDF, select the pages you want to remove and download a new PDF containing the remaining pages.

You can select individual pages such as 1, 3 and 7 or use ranges such as 4-8. The tool is designed for quick document cleanup, removing blank pages and preparing PDFs for sharing or submission.

How to Remove Pages from a PDF?

  1. Upload your PDF file.
  2. Check the total number of pages.
  3. Click the page numbers you want to delete.
  4. Alternatively, enter page numbers or ranges in the selection box.
  5. Click Remove Pages & Download.
  6. Your new PDF will be downloaded automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove pages from a PDF?

Upload your PDF, select the pages you want to remove and click Remove Pages & Download. A new PDF will be created with the remaining pages.

Can I remove multiple pages at once?

Yes. You can select multiple page numbers or enter multiple pages and ranges, such as 1,3,5-8.

Is the PDF page remover free?

Yes. Antokia's PDF Page Remover is free to use.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can use the tool without registration or login.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

The PDF processing is performed in your browser and does not require uploading the document to an Antokia conversion server.

Antokia provides browser-based utility tools for general document processing. Always review the generated PDF before using it for official, legal or business purposes.
