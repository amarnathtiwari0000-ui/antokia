Free Word to PDF Converter

Antokia's Word to PDF Converter helps you convert DOCX Word documents into PDF files directly from your browser. The tool is designed for resumes, assignments, reports, invoices, forms and other documents that need to be shared in PDF format.

No account or email is required. The conversion is performed in your browser, making the tool convenient for quick document conversion on desktop and mobile devices.

How to Convert Word to PDF?

  1. Select a DOCX Word file from your device.
  2. Wait for the document preview to load.
  3. Review the document preview.
  4. Click Convert to PDF.
  5. Your PDF file will be downloaded automatically.

Why Use Antokia Word to PDF?

  • Free to use.
  • No registration or login required.
  • No watermark added by the tool.
  • Browser-based document processing.
  • Simple drag-and-drop interface.
  • Works on desktop and mobile browsers.

Note: This version is designed for DOCX files. Complex Word documents containing advanced layouts, special fonts, floating objects or unsupported formatting may not reproduce exactly in the generated PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Word to PDF?

Select your DOCX file, review the document preview and click Convert to PDF. The generated PDF will be downloaded to your device.

Is this Word to PDF converter free?

Yes. The Antokia Word to PDF converter is available free of charge.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can use the tool without creating an account or providing an email address.

Are my documents uploaded?

The conversion is performed in your browser and does not require uploading the document to an Antokia conversion server.

Can I convert DOC files?

This version is designed for DOCX files. If you have an older DOC file, convert it to DOCX first and then use this tool.
