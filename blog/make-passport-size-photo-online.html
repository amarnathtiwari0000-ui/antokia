How to Make a Passport Size Photo Online for Free

Need a passport-size or ID photo for a form? Prepare it yourself: clean background, correct size and small file size.

Updated: November 2026 · Antokia Guide

Passport, visa, exam and ID forms usually ask for a photo with a clean background, a set size in pixels and a limit in KB. You can prepare it yourself on your phone or computer in a few minutes, without going to a studio. Here is the process step by step.

Important: Each office and form has its own rules for size, background colour, head position and file size. Always follow the official instructions for your application. The steps below show how to prepare a photo, not what the rules are.

What You Need

  • A clear photo taken in daylight or good light, facing the camera.
  • A plain light wall behind you, to make cutting the background easier.
  • The size rules of your form: width, height, KB limit and format.

Step-by-Step

Step 1: Take a good photo

Stand in front of a plain wall, keep the camera at eye level, look straight, and keep your face fully visible with no shadows. Do not use filters.

Step 2: Remove or replace the background

If the form needs a white background, use Background Remover to cut out the person. This works best on a clear, well lit photo.

Step 3: Resize to the required dimensions

Open Image Resizer and enter the width and height required. Keep the aspect ratio unless the form states exact dimensions.

Step 4: Compress to the KB limit

Use Image Compressor and reduce quality until the file is below the limit. Check that the face is still sharp.

Step 5: Check and upload

Confirm size, format (JPG or PNG), and that nothing important is cropped.

Common Reasons Photos Get Rejected

ReasonHow to avoid it
Shadows on face or backgroundUse soft, even light and stand away from the wall
Wrong size or aspect ratioResize to the exact dimensions in the notice
File too largeCompress after resizing
Blurry imageAvoid over-compression and zoomed-in crops
Glasses glare, hats or heavy filtersRemove them unless the rules allow
Tip: Take the photo at the highest quality first. You can always reduce size later, but you cannot add sharpness back.
