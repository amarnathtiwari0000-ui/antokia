How to Calculate Percentage? 3 Easy Tricks

Updated on Sep 20, 2026 • 3 min read • For Students & Shopping

Percentage nikalna har jagah kaam aata hai - marks nikalne me, discount nikalne me, GST nikalne me. Bahut logon ko iska formula yaad nahi rehta. Is blog me 3 sabse easy tarike bataunga jo Antokia ke Percentage Calculator me bhi hai.

1. What is X% of Y? (Sabse Common)

Formula: (X * Y) / 100

Example: 20% of 500 kya hoga?
(20 * 500) / 100 = 100

Jaise: Agar 500 me se 20% discount hai toh 100 Rs bachenge.

2. X is what % of Y? (Marks ke liye)

Formula: (X / Y) * 100

Example: Tumhe 400 me se 320 marks mile, toh percentage kya?
(320 / 400) * 100 = 80%

3. Discount Calculator (Shopping ke liye)

Formula: Saving = Price * Discount% / 100

Example: 2000 ki shirt pe 25% discount
Saving = 2000*25/100 = ₹500
Pay karna hai = ₹1500
