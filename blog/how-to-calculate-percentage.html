FAQ

Q. How to calculate discount?

If item is ₹1000 and 20% discount, Discount = 1000 * 20 / 100 = ₹200. Final Price = ₹800.

Q. How to calculate marks percentage?

Total marks obtained divided by total marks * 100. e.g. 450/500*100 = 90%.
