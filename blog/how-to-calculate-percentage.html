Maths & Percentage Guide

How to Calculate Percentage? Formula, Examples & Easy Methods

Learn how to calculate percentages with simple formulas and examples. Understand marks percentage, discounts, percentage increase and decrease without complicated calculations.

Easy Guide Percentage Calculator Antokia.com

Percentage is one of the most commonly used mathematical concepts in everyday life. We use percentages to calculate marks, discounts, GST, salary changes, profits, losses, interest and many other values.

The basic concept is simple: percentage means a value out of 100. Once you understand a few basic formulas, you can solve many percentage problems quickly.

What Does Percentage Mean?

The word percentage means "per hundred". It is represented using the % symbol.

Basic Percentage Formula
Percentage = (Part ÷ Total) × 100

1. How to Calculate X% of Y

To find a particular percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide the result by 100.

Example: What is 20% of 500?
20 ÷ 100 × 500
= 100

Therefore, 20% of 500 is 100.

2. How to Find What Percentage One Number Is of Another

When you want to know what percentage one number represents out of another number, divide the first number by the total and multiply by 100.

Formula
Percentage = (Part ÷ Total) × 100
Example: 45 is what percentage of 50?
(45 ÷ 50) × 100
= 90%

Therefore, 45 is 90% of 50.

3. How to Calculate Percentage Increase or Decrease

Percentage increase or decrease is useful when comparing an original value with a new value.

Percentage Change Formula
Percentage Change = ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100
Example: A price increases from ₹500 to ₹600.
Difference = ₹600 − ₹500 = ₹100
Percentage Increase = (100 ÷ 500) × 100
= 20%

How to Calculate Marks Percentage

Percentage is commonly used to calculate examination or school marks.

Marks Percentage Formula
(Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100
Example: 450 marks out of 500
(450 ÷ 500) × 100
= 90%

How to Calculate Discount Percentage

Percentage is also commonly used when shopping. A discount percentage tells you how much is reduced from the original price.

Example: ₹1,000 item with 20% discount
Discount = ₹1,000 × 20 ÷ 100
Discount = ₹200
Final Price = ₹800
