How to Calculate GST: Formula, Examples and Free Calculator

Need to add GST to a price or find the original price from a GST-inclusive bill? Here are the simple formulas with worked examples, including the 18% GST calculation.

Updated: October 2026 · Antokia Guide

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is charged as a percentage of the price. Once you know the rate, the calculation is just two small formulas, one for adding GST and one for removing it.

Quick answer: To add GST: GST = Price x Rate / 100, then Total = Price + GST. To remove GST: Price = Total / (1 + Rate/100). Or just use the free GST Calculator.

How to Add GST (Exclusive Price)

Formula

GST amount = Original price x GST rate / 100
Final price = Original price + GST amount

Example (18% GST): Price is Rs 10,000. GST = 10,000 x 18 / 100 = Rs 1,800. Final price = Rs 11,800.

How to Remove GST (Inclusive Price)

Formula

Original price = Total price / (1 + GST rate / 100)
GST amount = Total price - Original price

Example (18% GST): Total is Rs 11,800. Original price = 11,800 / 1.18 = Rs 10,000. GST = Rs 1,800.

Common mistake: Do not simply take 18% of the total to remove GST. 18% of Rs 11,800 is Rs 2,124, which is wrong. The right GST is Rs 1,800.

CGST, SGST and IGST

  • CGST + SGST: For sales within the same state, GST is split equally. For 18% GST, it is 9% CGST and 9% SGST.
  • IGST: For sales between two states, the full rate (for example 18%) is charged as IGST.

Example: On a Rs 10,000 sale within a state at 18%, CGST is Rs 900 and SGST is Rs 900.
