How to Calculate Age Accurately From Date of Birth

Published on Antokia.com - Free Tools for India

Government form, school admission, ya SSC / UPSC form me exact age chahiye hota hai. Manual calculation me galti ho jati hai, khaas kar leap year ki wajah se. Yahan sahi formula aur easy tarika samjho.

Formula to Calculate Age

Simple formula: Current Date - Date of Birth
Lekin isme 3 steps hote hain:

  • Years: Current Year - Birth Year
  • Months: Agar current month birth month se chhota hai, to 1 year kam kar do aur 12 months add karo
  • Days: Agar current day birth day se chhota hai, to 1 month kam karo aur us mahine ke din add karo

Example

DOB: 15th Aug 2000
Today: 17th Sep 2026
Result: 26 Years, 1 Month, 2 Days
