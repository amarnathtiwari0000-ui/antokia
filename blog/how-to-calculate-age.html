How to Calculate Age Accurately From Date of Birth

Published by Antokia - Free Online Tools

Calculating your exact age seems simple, but many people make mistakes when counting months and leap years. In this guide we will show you the correct formula and an easy automatic method.

What is Age Calculation?

Age is the time elapsed between your Date of Birth (DOB) and today's date. It is not just years, but also months and days. For school admission, government forms, and job applications, you need your exact age in Years, Months, Days.

Manual Formula to Calculate Age

Formula: Age = Current Date - Date of Birth

Example: If DOB is 15 Aug 2000 and Today is 16 Sep 2026

  • Years: 2026 - 2000 = 26 years, but birthday not completed this year? Check month.
  • Since September is after August, you have completed 26 years.
  • Months: 1 month (Aug 15 to Sep 15)
  • Days: 1 day extra
  • Final Age: 26 Years, 1 Month, 1 Day

Why Manual Calculation is Confusing?

1. Leap years have 29 days in February.
2. Months have different days (30, 31, 28).
3. If your birthday hasn't come this year, you need to subtract 1 year.
