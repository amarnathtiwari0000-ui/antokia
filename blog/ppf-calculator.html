What is PPF Calculator?

PPF Calculator is a tool that calculates your total maturity amount. You enter yearly investment, and it shows you Total Investment, Total Interest Earned, and Final Maturity Value after 15 years. For example, if you invest Rs 1.5 Lakh every year for 15 years at 7.1%, you will get approx Rs 40.68 Lakh! Out of that, Rs 18.18 Lakh is only interest. That's the power of compounding. Our calculator uses the exact government formula: F = P [({(1+i)^n}-1)/i] to give you 100% accurate result.

Why PPF is Best for Tax Saving?

PPF is EEE - Exempt, Exempt, Exempt. Your investment up to 1.5 Lakh is tax free under 80C, interest earned is tax free, and maturity amount is also tax free. No other investment gives you triple tax benefit. Plus it is fully backed by Government of India, so 100% safe. Better than FD. You can also extend PPF after 15 years in blocks of 5 years.
