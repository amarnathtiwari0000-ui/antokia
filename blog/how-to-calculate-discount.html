How to Calculate Discount: Formula, Examples & Easy Method

Learn how to calculate discount percentage, discount amount and final price with simple formulas and examples.

What is a Discount?

A discount is a reduction in the original price of a product or service. Discounts are commonly offered during sales, festivals, online shopping events and special promotions.

For example, if a product costs ₹1,000 and the seller offers a 20% discount, the customer pays less than the original price.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = (Original Price × Discount %) ÷ 100

Once you calculate the discount amount, subtract it from the original price to find the final selling price.

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

How to Calculate Discount – Example

Suppose a product has an original price of ₹1,000 and the discount is 20%.

First, calculate the discount amount:

Discount = (₹1,000 × 20) ÷ 100

Discount = ₹200

Now subtract the discount from the original price:

Final Price = ₹1,000 − ₹200 = ₹800

Therefore, after a 20% discount, the customer pays ₹800.

Example: 10% Discount

Suppose the original price of a product is ₹2,000 and the discount is 10%.

Discount = (₹2,000 × 10) ÷ 100 = ₹200

Final Price = ₹2,000 − ₹200 = ₹1,800

The customer saves ₹200 and pays ₹1,800.

Example: 30% Discount

Consider a product priced at ₹5,000 with a 30% discount.

Discount = (₹5,000 × 30) ÷ 100 = ₹1,500

Final Price = ₹5,000 − ₹1,500 = ₹3,500

Therefore, the customer saves ₹1,500 and pays ₹3,500.

How to Calculate Discount Percentage

If you know the original price and the amount saved, you can calculate the discount percentage using this formula:

Discount % = (Discount Amount ÷ Original Price) × 100

For example, if a product originally costs ₹2,000 and you save ₹400:

Discount % = (400 ÷ 2,000) × 100

Discount % = 20%

How to Find the Original Price After Discount

Sometimes you know the final price and discount percentage but want to find the original price.

Original Price = Final Price ÷ (1 − Discount Rate)

For example, if the final price is ₹800 after a 20% discount:

Original Price = ₹800 ÷ 0.80

Original Price = ₹1,000

Discount Amount vs Final Price

It is important to understand the difference between the discount amount and the final price.

  • Original Price: Price before discount.
  • Discount Amount: Money saved because of the discount.
  • Final Price: Amount paid after subtracting the discount.

Common Discount Calculation Mistakes

  • Calculating the discount percentage on the wrong price.
  • Forgetting to divide the percentage by 100.
  • Confusing discount amount with final selling price.
  • Subtracting the discount percentage directly from the price without calculating the discount amount.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the basic discount formula?

The basic formula is: Discount Amount = (Original Price × Discount Percentage) ÷ 100.

How do I calculate a 20% discount?

Multiply the original price by 20 and divide by 100. For example, 20% of ₹1,000 is ₹200.

How do I calculate the final price after discount?

Subtract the discount amount from the original price. For example, if the original price is ₹1,000 and the discount is ₹200, the final price is ₹800.

How do I calculate discount percentage?

Divide the discount amount by the original price and multiply the result by 100.

Can I calculate discounts online?

Yes. You can use an online discount calculator to calculate the discount amount and final price quickly.

Conclusion

Calculating a discount is simple when you know the basic formula. First calculate the discount amount using the original price and discount percentage, then subtract it from the original price to find the final price.
