Old vs New Tax Regime 2026-27: Which One Should You Choose?

Confused between the old and new income tax regime? This guide compares slabs, deductions and tax on a real example so you can decide with confidence.

Updated: October 2026 · Antokia Guide

India has two income tax regimes. The new regime has lower rates but allows very few deductions. The old regime has higher rates but lets you reduce tax through deductions like 80C, 80D, HRA and home loan interest. Which one saves more depends on how much you can actually claim.

Quick answer: If your total deductions are small, the new regime usually wins. If you claim large deductions (home loan, HRA, 80C, 80D, NPS), the old regime can win. The only reliable way is to calculate both, which you can do free with the Income Tax Calculator.
Note: From 1 April 2026 the Income Tax Act, 2025 uses the term "Tax Year" instead of "Financial Year" and "Assessment Year". The slabs below are the ones shown on the official income tax portal when this guide was written. Always confirm on incometax.gov.in before filing.

The standard deduction is Rs 75,000 for salaried people. A section 87A rebate of up to Rs 60,000 applies if taxable income is up to Rs 12 lakh, so tax is zero up to that level (about Rs 12.75 lakh salary for a salaried person). Add 4% health and education cess on the tax.

Old Regime Slabs (below 60 years)

The standard deduction is Rs 50,000. The rebate is Rs 12,500, so there is no tax if taxable income is up to Rs 5 lakh. Senior citizens get a higher basic exemption (Rs 3 lakh at 60+, Rs 5 lakh at 80+).

Assume a salaried person with Rs 15,00,000 income. Tax below includes 4% cess and is calculated with the slabs above.

How to Decide in 4 Steps

Step 1: Add up your real deductions

Count only what you actually pay: 80C investments, health insurance, HRA, home loan interest and NPS.

Step 2: Calculate both regimes

Use the Income Tax Calculator with the same income for both.

Step 3: Compare the final tax

Pick the regime with the lower tax. If the difference is small, the simpler new regime may save you paperwork.

Step 4: Tell your employer and file correctly

Inform your employer of your choice for TDS, and select the same regime while filing your ITR. Keep your documents ready as PDFs, and use Compress PDF if a portal limits file size.

Common Mistakes

  • Choosing a regime only because of last year's habit.
  • Counting deductions that you have not actually invested in or paid.
  • Forgetting that HRA and 80C do not work in the new regime.
  • Ignoring surcharge and cess when comparing.

This guide is for general information, not tax advice. Rules and slabs can change, so verify with the official income tax portal or a tax professional.
