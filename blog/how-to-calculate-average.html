How to Calculate Average: Formula, Examples & Easy Method

Learn how to calculate the average of numbers using a simple formula with easy examples and a step-by-step method.

Easy Guide Average Formula Antokia.com

Calculating an average is one of the most common mathematical tasks used in school, college, business and everyday life. Average can be used to find the typical value of a group of numbers.

For example, you may want to calculate the average marks of students, average monthly expenses, average salary, average temperature or the average of several test scores.

What Is an Average?

In basic mathematics, the average usually means the arithmetic mean. It is calculated by adding all the numbers together and then dividing the total by the number of values.

Average Formula
Average = Sum of All Values ÷ Number of Values

How to Calculate Average Step by Step

Step 1: Add All the Numbers

First, add every number in the group to find the total sum.

Step 2: Count the Numbers

Count how many numbers are included in the calculation. This gives you the number of values.

Step 3: Divide the Total by the Number of Values

Finally, divide the total sum by the number of values. The result is the average.

Example: Calculate the Average of 5 Numbers

Numbers
10, 20, 30, 40, 50
Step 1: Add the numbers
10 + 20 + 30 + 40 + 50 = 150
Step 2: Count the numbers
5 numbers
Step 3: Divide
150 ÷ 5 = 30
Average
30

Average of Marks

Average is commonly used to calculate the average marks obtained by a student in several subjects.

For example, if a student gets 70, 80, 75, 85 and 90 marks, the average can be calculated by adding all five marks and dividing the total by 5.

Example
(70 + 80 + 75 + 85 + 90) ÷ 5 = 80
Average Marks = 80

Why Is Average Useful?

Average helps summarize a group of numbers using one representative value. It can make large sets of data easier to understand and compare.

  • Useful for calculating average marks.
  • Helps compare test scores.
  • Useful for calculating average expenses.
  • Can be used for average salary calculations.
  • Useful for analysing numerical data.

Important Point About Average

Remember:

The average is calculated by dividing the total sum by the number of values. Make sure every value is included when calculating the sum and count.
