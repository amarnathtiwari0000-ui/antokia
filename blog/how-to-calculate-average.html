How to Calculate Average: Formula, Examples & Easy Method

Learn how to calculate average easily with simple formulas and practical examples.

What is Average?

An average is a single value that represents the typical or central value of a group of numbers. It is commonly used in mathematics, education, business, finance and everyday calculations.

The most commonly used type of average is the arithmetic mean. It is calculated by adding all the numbers together and dividing the total by the number of values.

Average Formula

Average = Sum of all values ÷ Number of values

In simple words, add all the numbers and divide the result by how many numbers are present.

How to Calculate Average

Let's take a simple example. Suppose you have the following five numbers:

10, 20, 30, 40, 50

First, add all the numbers:

10 + 20 + 30 + 40 + 50 = 150

There are 5 numbers, so divide the total by 5:

Average = 150 ÷ 5 = 30

Therefore, the average of these five numbers is 30.

Example: Average of 3 Numbers

Suppose the numbers are:

15, 25 and 35

Add the numbers:

15 + 25 + 35 = 75

There are 3 numbers:

Average = 75 ÷ 3 = 25

Therefore, the average is 25.

How to Calculate Average Marks

Average marks can be calculated by adding the marks obtained in different subjects and dividing the total by the number of subjects.

For example, suppose a student gets the following marks:

Subject Marks
Mathematics 80
Science 70
English 90

Total = 80 + 70 + 90 = 240

Average = 240 ÷ 3 = 80

The average marks are therefore 80.

How to Calculate Average Salary

The same formula can be used to calculate the average salary of a group of employees.

For example, suppose three employees earn:

₹30,000, ₹40,000 and ₹50,000

Average Salary = (30,000 + 40,000 + 50,000) ÷ 3

Average Salary = ₹40,000

How to Calculate Average of Many Numbers

When there are many numbers, the process remains the same:

  1. Add all the numbers.
  2. Count how many numbers are present.
  3. Divide the total by the number of values.

Example: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30

Sum = 105

Number of values = 6

Average = 105 ÷ 6 = 17.5

Important Things to Remember

  • Always add all the values before dividing.
  • Count the exact number of values.
  • Divide the total by the number of values.
  • The average does not always have to be a whole number.
  • Decimal values can also be an average.

Common Mistakes When Calculating Average

  • Forgetting to include one of the numbers in the total.
  • Dividing by the wrong number of values.
  • Adding numbers incorrectly.
  • Confusing average with the highest or lowest value.
