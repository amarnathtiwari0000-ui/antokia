Privacy & Security

The Page Numbering tool processes your PDF in the browser using client-side JavaScript. Antokia does not intentionally upload your file to its servers for this operation. However, always exercise caution with sensitive documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the PDF uploaded to your server?
No — the tool runs in your browser. The selected file is processed locally unless you explicitly use a server-side feature.
Can I control which pages get numbered?
Yes — you can specify a page range (for example 2-5) or number the entire document.
Will page numbering change the PDF quality?
No — adding page numbers only overlays text on pages and preserves the original content and resolution.
