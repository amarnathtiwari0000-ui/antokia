How Much SIP Do You Need to Reach Rs 1 Crore?

Want to build Rs 1 crore with a monthly SIP? See the amount needed for 10, 15, 20 and 25 years at different assumed returns.

Updated: November 2026 · Antokia Guide

Rs 1 crore sounds like a big target, but a monthly SIP and time can get you there. The key is how long you invest and what return you assume. This guide shows the monthly amount needed for different periods using a standard SIP formula.

Example: At an assumed 12% yearly return, you would need about Rs 10,100 per month for 20 years to reach Rs 1 crore. Your own money put in would be about Rs 24,24,000.

Monthly SIP Needed for Rs 1 Crore

These figures assume a fixed yearly return, monthly investing at the start of each month and no step-up. Real returns change every year.

10 yearsRs 48,500Rs 43,100Rs 35,900

The pattern is clear: starting earlier reduces the monthly amount a lot. Doubling the time does much more than doubling the amount.

How the Calculation Works

A SIP grows by compounding. Each monthly instalment earns returns, and those returns also earn returns. The future value is found with this formula:

Future value = P x [((1 + i)^n - 1) / i] x (1 + i)
P = monthly amount, i = monthly rate (yearly rate / 12 / 100), n = number of months

You do not need to do this by hand. Put your amount, rate and years into the SIP Calculator.

Ways to Reach the Goal Faster

  • Start now: every year of delay raises the monthly amount you need.
  • Step up your SIP: increase the amount by 5 to 10 percent each year as your income grows.
  • Stay invested: stopping during a market fall can hurt the result.
  • Keep costs low: check the expense ratio of the fund you choose.

Important Things to Remember

  • Returns of mutual funds are market-linked and are not guaranteed. The table is an illustration, not a promise.
  • Inflation reduces what Rs 1 crore can buy in the future, so you may need a bigger goal.
  • Taxes on gains can apply, depending on the type of fund and holding period.
  • Read more in SIP vs mutual fund before you choose.
