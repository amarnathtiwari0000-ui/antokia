QR Code Ideas for Small Business: 8 Practical Uses

QR codes can save time and printing costs for small businesses. Here are 8 practical uses and how to make a code that scans well.

Updated: November 2026 · Antokia Guide

A QR code is a square barcode that a phone camera can scan. It can open a website, show a phone number, share Wi-Fi details or take someone to a map location. A small business can use QR codes to save time, reduce printing costs and make it easy for customers to reach them.

Practical QR Code Ideas

IdeaHow to use it
Website or shop pagePrint the QR on a visiting card, board or bill so people open your page quickly

Tips for QR Codes That Scan Well

  • Print it large enough, at least about 2 cm by 2 cm for close scanning, and bigger for posters.
  • Use dark code on a light background, with a clear empty margin around it.
  • Do not stretch or distort it.
  • Add a short line of text near it, like "Scan to see menu", so people know why to scan.
  • Use a link that does not change. A QR code cannot be edited after printing.

Safety

Only scan QR codes from trusted places, and never enter passwords or payment details on a page you reached by a code you do not trust. If you place a QR code on a poster, check that no one has stuck another code over it.
