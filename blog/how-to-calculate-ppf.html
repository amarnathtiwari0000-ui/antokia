What is PPF?

PPF stands for Public Provident Fund. It is a long-term savings scheme designed for individuals who want to build savings over a longer period.

PPF calculations depend on factors such as the amount deposited, timing of deposits, applicable interest rate and investment period. The standard PPF account has a 15-year maturity period, subject to the applicable PPF rules.

Important: PPF interest rates are notified by the Government of India and may change from time to time. When estimating your returns, always use the applicable rate for the relevant period.

How is PPF Interest Calculated?

PPF interest is calculated according to the rules of the PPF scheme and is compounded annually. The amount and timing of deposits can affect the interest earned during the year.

For a simple yearly investment illustration, a commonly used estimation formula is:

F = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i]

This formula is a simplified illustration for regular yearly contributions and should not be treated as the exact bank/post-office calculation for every deposit pattern.

  • F = Estimated future value
  • P = Regular yearly investment
  • i = Annual interest rate in decimal form
  • n = Number of years

Example: ₹1.5 Lakh Investment for 15 Years

Suppose you invest ₹1,50,000 every year for 15 years and use 7.1% as the assumed annual interest rate for illustration.

Particular Example Value
Yearly Investment ₹1,50,000
Investment Period 15 Years
Assumed Interest Rate 7.1%
Total Contributions ₹22,50,000
Estimated Future Value* Approx. ₹40.68 Lakh

*This is an illustrative estimate based on a simplified annual contribution calculation. Actual PPF interest depends on deposit timing and the applicable scheme rules and rate.

Important Things to Remember About PPF

  • The standard PPF maturity period is 15 years, subject to applicable rules.
  • The interest rate is notified by the Government and can change over time.
  • Deposit timing can affect the interest calculation.
  • The annual contribution limit should be checked against the current PPF rules.
  • Calculator results are estimates and should be checked against official scheme rules.
Note: Do not assume that a historical PPF interest rate will remain unchanged for the complete investment period. For financial planning, use the applicable rate and verify current PPF rules before making investment decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the PPF interest rate?
PPF interest rates are notified by the Government of India and can change from time to time. Always check the applicable rate before calculating your expected returns.
How is PPF interest calculated?
PPF interest is calculated according to the PPF scheme rules and compounded annually. The timing and amount of deposits can affect the interest earned.
What is the PPF maturity period?
The standard PPF account has a maturity period of 15 years from the end of the financial year in which the account was opened, subject to the applicable rules.
