What is a PDF to Word Converter?

A PDF to Word converter changes readable text from a PDF document into a Microsoft Word DOCX file. This can make text easier to edit, reuse and format.

How to Convert PDF to Word

  1. Click the Choose PDF File button.
  2. Select your PDF document.
  3. Click Convert PDF to Word.
  4. Wait for the conversion to finish.
  5. Click Download Word File.

Important Limitation

This tool extracts selectable text from PDF files. Image-only scanned PDFs may require OCR and may not produce fully editable text. Complex layouts, tables, images and advanced PDF formatting may also not be reproduced exactly.
