What is a PDF to Word Converter?

A PDF to Word Converter converts text available inside a PDF document into a Word DOCX file. This can make extracted document content easier to edit, reuse and format.

Antokia's PDF to Word Converter is designed for supported text-based PDF documents and works directly in the browser.
