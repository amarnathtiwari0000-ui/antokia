What is a PDF to Word Converter?

A PDF to Word Converter extracts available text from a PDF document and creates a Word DOCX file. This makes supported PDF content easier to edit, reuse and format.

Antokia's PDF to Word Converter works directly in your browser and is designed for text-based PDF documents.
