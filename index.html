Welcome to Antokia

Welcome to Antokia.com, a free online tools platform created to make common digital tasks simpler and more accessible. Our goal is to provide practical browser-based tools with clean interfaces that are easy to understand and convenient to use.

Antokia was started in 2026 in Mumbai, India with the idea of creating useful digital utilities for students, professionals, creators, businesses and everyday internet users around the world.

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to make useful online utilities easier to access. We focus on straightforward tools that help people complete everyday digital tasks from a web browser.

Privacy & Browser-Based Processing

Some Antokia tools are designed to process files directly in your browser. When a tool works entirely on your device, the file does not need to be uploaded to a server for processing. The exact behavior can vary by tool, so users should review the relevant tool page for specific information.
