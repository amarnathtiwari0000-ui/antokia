What is an Image to PDF Converter?

An Image to PDF Converter is an online tool that converts image files such as JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP into PDF documents. It can also combine multiple images into a single PDF file.

This can be useful when you need to combine scanned pages, photographs, receipts, certificates, forms or other image-based documents into one convenient PDF file.

Antokia's Image to PDF Converter is designed for simple browser-based conversion without requiring additional software or registration.
