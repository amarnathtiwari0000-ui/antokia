Frequently Asked Questions

Which tax regime is better, old or new?

It depends on your deductions. The new regime has lower rates but fewer deductions. If 80C, HRA, home loan interest and 80D together are large, the old regime may save more. Use the comparison above.

Is income up to 12 lakh tax free in the new regime?

For resident individuals, taxable income up to ₹12 lakh gets full rebate under 87A, so tax is nil. With the ₹75,000 standard deduction, salary up to ₹12.75 lakh is effectively tax free.

Which deductions work in the new regime?

Mainly the standard deduction and employer NPS contribution under 80CCD(2). Most other deductions are not available.
