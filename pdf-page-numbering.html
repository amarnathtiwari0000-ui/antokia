Add Page Numbers to a PDF

Antokia's PDF Page Numbering tool lets you add page numbers to your document quickly and easily in your browser. You can choose the number position, page range, font size, start number, and optional prefix or suffix to match your document formatting.

This tool works entirely on the client side, so your PDF stays in your browser while it is processed locally.

How to Number a PDF

1

Upload your PDF

Click the upload area or drag and drop a PDF document.

2

Choose the numbering options

Pick the page position, range, start number, font size, and prefix or suffix.

3

Apply page numbers

The page numbering is applied locally in your browser.

4

Download the file

Save the numbered PDF when processing is complete.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add page numbers to a PDF for free?

Yes. Antokia provides this PDF numbering tool free to use.

Is my PDF uploaded to Antokia?

No. The processing is carried out in your browser using JavaScript, so your file stays local.

Can I change the position of the page numbers?

Yes. You can set numbers at the bottom center, bottom right, top center, or top right of each page.
