What is PPF?

Public Provident Fund (PPF) is a long-term savings scheme. The National Savings Institute states that a PPF account has a minimum deposit of ₹500 and a maximum deposit of ₹1,50,000 in a financial year.

The standard maturity period is fifteen complete financial years from the end of the financial year in which the account is opened. The applicable interest rate is subject to government notification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the current PPF interest rate?

The default rate used on this page is 7.1%. PPF interest rates are government-notified and may change for different periods.

What is the maximum amount I can deposit in PPF?

The maximum PPF deposit is ₹1,50,000 in a financial year.

What is the minimum PPF deposit?

The minimum deposit is ₹500 in a financial year.

What is the standard PPF maturity period?

A PPF account matures after completion of fifteen complete financial years from the end of the financial year in which the account was opened.

Is the PPF calculator result exact?

No. It is an estimate. Actual PPF interest depends on the timing and pattern of deposits, applicable interest rates and scheme rules.

Can I change the interest rate?

Yes. The interest rate field is editable so you can use the calculator for comparison or different assumptions.
