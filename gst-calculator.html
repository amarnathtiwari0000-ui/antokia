How to Calculate GST

  • Add GST: GST = price × rate ÷ 100. Total = price + GST.
  • Remove GST: Original price = inclusive price × 100 ÷ (100 + rate).

Example: ₹1,000 at 18% GST gives GST of ₹180 and a total of ₹1,180. Removing 18% GST from ₹1,180 gives back ₹1,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate GST on a price?

Multiply the price by the GST rate and divide by 100. Add that to the price to get the total.

What are CGST and SGST?

For sales within one state, GST is split equally into CGST (central) and SGST (state). For sales between states, IGST is charged at the full rate.
