What is a Percentage?

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. The percentage symbol (%) is commonly used to describe discounts, increases, decreases, marks, interest, profit, loss and many other everyday calculations.

Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

To calculate a percentage of a number:

X% of Y = (X × Y) ÷ 100
