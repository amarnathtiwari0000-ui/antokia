Free ATS-Friendly Resume Maker

Antokia Resume Maker helps you create a simple and professional resume directly in your browser. You can enter your personal information, professional summary, skills, work experience, education, projects and certifications, then preview the finished resume before saving it.

What is an ATS-Friendly Resume?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software commonly used by employers to organize and screen job applications. A straightforward resume structure can make important information easier for automated systems and recruiters to identify. This resume maker uses a one-column layout, standard headings, readable text and avoids unnecessary tables, graphics and decorative elements.

How to Use Antokia Resume Maker

  1. Enter your full name and contact information.
  2. Add a concise professional summary.
  3. List relevant skills using clear keywords.
  4. Add your work experience and achievements.
  5. Add education, projects and certifications if relevant.
  6. Review the live resume preview.
  7. Click Print / Save PDF and select PDF in your browser's print options.

Tips for Creating a Better Resume

  • Keep your resume clear and easy to scan.
  • Use standard section headings such as Work Experience, Education and Skills.
  • Use measurable achievements where possible.
  • Use keywords that are relevant to the job description.
  • Avoid unnecessary graphics, photos, charts and decorative elements for ATS-focused applications.
  • Check spelling, dates, job titles and contact information before applying.
  • Keep your resume focused on relevant professional information.
