What is an ATS-Friendly Resume?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software that employers may use to collect, organize and search information from job applications. An ATS-friendly resume generally uses clear headings, readable text and a straightforward structure.

Antokia Resume Maker uses a simple text-based resume structure with standard sections such as Professional Summary, Work Experience, Education and Skills. This design is intended to keep important resume information easy to read and identify.

ATS-Friendly Resume Tips

Use clear headings

Use recognizable sections such as Work Experience, Education and Skills.

Use relevant keywords

Review the job description and use relevant skills and terminology when they accurately describe your experience.

Keep formatting simple

Avoid unnecessary graphics, decorative elements and complex layouts when preparing an ATS-focused resume.

Check for errors

Review spelling, dates, company names, job titles and contact information before submitting your resume.

What Should a Resume Include?

Contact Information: Include your name, professional title, email and phone number.

Professional Summary: Provide a short overview of your experience and professional strengths.

Work Experience: List relevant roles, employers, dates and responsibilities or achievements.

Education: Add relevant qualifications, institutions and years.

Skills: Include relevant technical and professional skills.
